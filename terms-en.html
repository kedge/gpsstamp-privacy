Using the app

GPS Stamp provides local camera, watermark and media-management tools. Version 1.0.5 has no advertising, subscriptions or in-app purchases. No online account is required.

Your content

You control captured and imported media, watermark text, logos and sharing destinations. Obtain the rights and permissions needed to photograph people or sites and to use imported content. The app does not publish your media to an operator-owned feed.

Time and location

Watermarks use device time, the selected time zone and available location information. These values can be inaccurate or unavailable and are not an independent authenticity certification. Verify information when you need a reliable record.

Originals and copies

Private photo originals may expire or be deleted under your retention settings. Re-editing and privacy export require an available original. Video watermarks are permanently embedded, and unstamped video originals are not retained. Keep backups of important files.

Privacy

The privacy policy explains local records, optional location and microphone access, system address lookup, sharing and deletion. Contact the operator listed above for support. Your applicable mandatory consumer and privacy rights are not excluded by these terms.
